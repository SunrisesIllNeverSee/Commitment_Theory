## Lens Patent Search

Once the patent is indexed in The Lens:

1. Search Lens by Serial No. 63/877,177 OR by inventor name
2. Claim into your Lens Profile (researcher + inventor combined profile)
3. Verify it appears under "Patents" on your ORCID record (orcid.org/[your-iD])

If the provisional doesn't appear in Lens within 30 days of filing, search again — provisional patent applications sometimes lag in public databases.

## Update Protocol

Update this file whenever:

- A new patent is filed (add row to Active Filings + new Prosecution Timeline section)
