### L-001 — SLRO Essay (submitted May 1, 2026)

- *Heppner* (SDNY): criminal defendant's AI-generated materials not protected (no counsel direction)
- *Warner* (E.D. Mich.): pro se plaintiff's AI-assisted materials protected (she is "simultaneously the party and the advocate")

The Conservation Law enters in Section VI as "materials for building" — verification infrastructure that makes the functional rule's protection demonstrable, not merely doctrinal. Cryptographic lineage, fidelity verification gates, recursion testing. "Did the protection survive the pipeline?"

### The CCR (Commitment Conservation Requirement)
